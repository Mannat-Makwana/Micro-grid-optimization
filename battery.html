Battery role in the optimization

The optimizer uses the battery to shift renewable energy across time while maintaining the configured reserve. Discharge can reduce diesel dependence during high-demand periods; charging can absorb renewable energy that would otherwise be curtailed.

SOC trajectory is planned mock data. Actual battery telemetry will come from the future microgrid data feed.
